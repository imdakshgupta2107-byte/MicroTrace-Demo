What is included

  • Daily opening/closing controls, FEFO-safe guidance and issue-eligible stock handling.
  • Dynamic, grouped mass, volume and concentration selectors—from very small SI scales through customary and industrial units.
  • Decimal-normalized calculations for media preparation, molarity, C₁V₁ dilution, percentage solutions, CFU plating and cell seeding.

Do not treat calculated preparation values as a substitute for approved labels, SOPs, safety data, calibration or independent verification.

MicroTrace does not claim to eliminate every human loophole. It makes workflow steps and their limits visible; laboratories remain responsible for their approved procedures and controls.
